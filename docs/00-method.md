# The method

## The flip

| Traditional insurance | This platform |
|---|---|
| Pick a cover → get a price | Pick your price → AI builds the protection |
| Annual contract | Monthly, change plan any month |
| Meets you at purchase and at claim | Meets you every month |
| Pays after the loss | Prevents, rewards, and pays automatically |

## The model: an AI subscription

| AI subscription | This platform | Pain points fixed |
|---|---|---|
| A few fixed plans (Free, Pro, Max) | Starter HK$40 … Max HK$150 | C2, C3 |
| Monthly, cancel any time | No annual lock-in; change plan any month | C3, C7 |
| Use what you need within your plan | You split your price across covers each month (the optimiser suggests a split); no change carries last month's split over | C4, B5 |
| Value every month, not only in emergencies | Prevention tasks, rewards, Protection Score | C6, B2 |
| Lives inside tools you already use | Embedded in partner apps | B1, B6 |

## The loop

| Stage | What happens | Mechanism | Pains hit |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Understand | AI reads the customer's answers and upcoming events (for example a concert in three months) | M4 | C2, C7 |
| 2 Protect | Each cover is re-rated for the coming months' risk; the optimiser suggests a schedule inside the chosen price; AI explains it; customer adjusts and taps, or last month's split renews automatically | M2 | C3, C4, B5 |
| 3 Prevent | AI writes a prevention task for this specific event or risk | M6 | B3, C8 |
| 4 Verify | AI checks the photo or screenshot; people audit a random sample | M4 | B4, B9 |
| 5 Reward | AI recommends the reward this person will value, often a partner's spare capacity | M7 | C6, P1, P2 |
| 6 Recover | A trigger matches an external event to the cover and pays automatically; or an AI-assessed claim; or partner settlement in kind | M5 | C1, C5 |
| 7 Learn | Every verified prevention activity improves pricing, prevention tasks and rewards | M9 | B7, B2 |

## The prevention engine

```
unique event or risk
  → identify controllable causes
  → generate a relevant prevention action
  → choose a verifiable action
  → assign a relevant reward
  → verify completion
  → update protection and reward
```

Example tasks (phone case photo, battery scan, typhoon home checklist, helmet check-in) are
examples of output, not a fixed library.

A prevention task belongs to the class of business of the cover it protects, and its reward and score improve
terms only within that group (R36): a helmet check-in counts for accident cover, never for
device cover. Prevention tasks target loss-based covers only; automatic covers pay on an external
trigger that behaviour cannot change, so they move only with risk (R38).

## Better protection

Better does not mean more insurance. It means protecting what the customer actually cares
about, at the moment it matters, inside the budget they chose.
