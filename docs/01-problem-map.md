## Who it is for

People outside the traditional insurance funnel: those who distrust insurance, have low or
unstable income, won't study complex products, see no relevance in traditional cover, or won't
commit to a year. Gig workers are the first use case, not the market.

Four barriers removed: **financial** (small flexible budget), **cognitive** (plain explanation),
**relevance** (covers what they care about now), **value** (rewards even when no loss happens).
