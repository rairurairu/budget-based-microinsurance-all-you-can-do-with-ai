# Business rules

These are the method. Adopters change parameters in `config/`, never these rules. Every rule
has a test named `rXX_*` (Rust: `crates/engine/tests/rules.rs`, or where a boundary enforces it,
`crates/api/tests`, `crates/learning/tests`, `crates/ai/tests`) or `test_rXX_*` (Python).

Source tags: **[IDEA]** ideation map · **[PLAN]** first build plan · **[DEF]** design review notes ·
**[DER]** derived engineering rule · **[OWN]** product owner decision (2026-10).

| ID | Rule | Source | Enforced in |
|---|---|---|---|
| R01 | The customer chooses one of a fixed set of plans. The plan price is the same for everyone and changes only by the customer's choice or a promotional discount (R44); what each plan buys is risk-rated (R31) | IDEA, OWN | engine::plans |
| R02 | AI never sets prices, rates or sums insured. The customer allocates the plan price (R34); a deterministic, unit-tested optimiser proposes an allocation and converts premium into sums insured | IDEA, OWN | engine::optimiser |
| R03 | Nothing the customer changes takes effect without their tap to confirm. With no change, the previous allocation renews automatically (R34) | PLAN, OWN | engine::consent |
| R04 | Changes take effect from the next policy period, never mid-event | PLAN | engine::consent |
| R05 | Minimum 1-month subscription per cover | PLAN | engine::consent |
| R06 | No new weather cover once a tropical cyclone signal (Signal 1 or above in Hong Kong) is in force; each cover lists the event types and stages that stop new cover, in config (R55) | PLAN, OWN | engine::catalogue |
| R07 | 14-day waiting period on device cover | PLAN | engine::catalogue, engine::claims |
| R08 | Parametric covers pay on the trigger during declared working hours, not on proven loss. The disruption runs from the trigger until the cover's `resumption_minutes` after it ends; a day counts once `min_disrupted_minutes` of declared work fall in it, and a daily benefit is paid at most once per day (ADR-006) | PLAN, OWN | engine::triggers |
| R09 | Parametric payout sized below typical daily earnings | PLAN | engine::catalogue |
| R10 | Points and rewards only for verified prevention activities, never self-reported ones. A registered measure earns once per its `repeat_after_days` per customer; evidence is sent by the task's due date; suspected reuse or edits go to a person, who alone decides fraud (ADR-009); a reward is voided only on that finding (ADR-010) | PLAN, DER | engine::prevention, engine::score, ai::verify |
| R11 | Points earn a premium rebate (credited after the policy period; the plan price is unchanged) or a bonus sum insured, as the customer chooses; never makes terms worse than the policy period's risk-rated base (R31); effect capped at 20% per points pool (R36) | PLAN, OWN | engine::score |
| R12 | Points fade after about three months | PLAN | engine::score |
| R13 | AI may approve or refer a claim, never decline; people decide declines. The assessor's reply and the straight-through outcome have no decline; a person declines with a reason from a closed list tied to the wording (ADR-010) | IDEA | engine::claims, ai::assess |
| R14 | A person randomly audits 1 in 20 automated decisions, and every decision whose anomaly score reaches `risk_review_threshold`. The sample is drawn from the decision id and the insurer's secret seed, recorded so it can be replayed. A verified prevention activity drawn is credited only after the person confirms it; a payout is audited after payment (ADR-009). A claim drawn, or at the threshold, is paid only after the person decides. A claim's anomaly score is the sum of its configured fraud indicators, each named (`claims.anomaly`); the random sample's overturn rate is reported per decision kind (ADR-010) | IDEA, DER | engine::guardrails, engine::prevention, engine::claims |
| R15 | Any AI decision can be appealed to a person. Any decision by the AI or the rules can be put to a person on the customer's request, and a person's adverse decision (a decline) to a different person; each is decided within `appeal_sla_hours`. The customer's own choices are changed by them, not reviewed (ADR-010) | IDEA, DER | engine::guardrails, engine::claims, engine::prevention |
| R16 | Cash is always offered next to partner rewards and settlement in kind; customer chooses. A reward balance can always be taken in cash in full, first on the menu | IDEA | engine::rewards, engine::payouts, ai::rewards |
| R17 | Monthly reward cap bounds exposure: rewards granted to a customer in one calendar month of market local time, prevention and loyalty together, stay within `monthly_cap_per_customer`; a grant over the room left is reduced and the customer is told | IDEA | engine::rewards |
| R18 | A prevention task type is allowed only if it is a registered loss prevention measure (`config/prevention_measures.yaml`) and reward cost < claim probability reduction × average claim cost, using the claims data of the prevention task's own class of business (R36), from the prevention basis (ADR-009) | PLAN, OWN | engine::config, engine::prevention, ai::prevention, python/economics prevention_test |
| R19 | Age, gender and district may be rating factors in engine rating where the industry uses them and they are registered for that class of business (R33); ethnicity, religion and nationality never. None of them is ever an AI input; all remain in the fairness audit: per decision kind and audit attribute, a credible group (at least `min_group_decisions`) whose approval rate is below `min_parity_ratio` of the best credible group's is flagged for people to investigate (adverse impact ratio; ADR-010) | IDEA, OWN | engine::config (factor registry), engine::guardrails, ai::harness |
| R20 | Per-data-type opt-in; data used only for the cover it supports; optional data deleted on cancellation (R54); prevention works without location | PLAN | engine::consent, learning, ai::prevention |
| R21 | Partners receive consented, aggregated insights only; personal data never sold. A figure computed over fewer than `channels.partner_insights_min_cohort` customers is withheld, never estimated (ADR-011) | IDEA | learning::insights, api::partners |
| R22 | Every AI reply is schema-checked; slow, failed or invalid → safe saved answer | IDEA | ai::harness |
| R23 | Every recommendation is explained in one plain sentence | PLAN | ai::explain |
| R24 | Only pre-approved products of a licensed insurer are bundled | PLAN | engine::catalogue |
| R25 | Identity checks go to a licensed eKYC provider | IDEA | api::identity |
| R26 | Rewards recognise sustained behaviour and participation; no reward is conditional on not claiming. A loyalty reward follows `loyalty.streak_periods` consecutive policy periods with a verified prevention activity in one points pool; what a reward can be earned for is a closed type with no claim-free variant. Claims experience enters terms only through each policy period's re-underwriting of the risk class (R39), as at any renewal | DEF, OWN | engine::rewards |
| R27 | One payout per (policy, loss occurrence, stage) (R51); payouts are idempotent | DER | engine::payouts, ledger |
| R28 | Settlement in kind only where suitable, permitted by product terms, delivered by a registered supplier (`config/suppliers.yaml`) and accepted by the customer. At a fixed tariff it replaces a cash amount only if its retail value is at least that amount and the tariff is not above it (fair value) | DEF, DER | engine::payouts, engine::config |
| R29 | Money is integer minor units; no floating-point arithmetic in money paths | DER | engine (clippy `float_arithmetic = deny`) |
| R30 | The engine has no I/O, clock reads, randomness or AI; same input, same output | DER | engine (dependency allow-list) |
| R31 | Each policy period, the sum insured and benefit amount per trigger event that each premium HK$ buys are re-rated from expected claim cost: rising risk buys less, falling risk buys more. A period takes the rates in force on its first day; a rating basis entry is in force for one policy period at most (ADR-011). The system never changes the plan price; only the customer chooses it (R01) | OWN | engine::optimiser, python/economics trigger_pricing |
| R32 | A policy period's rate uses the average expected claim cost over the next `rating_horizon_periods` policy periods (default 3), so known future risk shows early. For a customer with fewer than `continuous_cover_periods` continuous periods in that class (new or re-entering), the rate is the higher of that average and the period's own cost, so a peak is never rated down by calmer periods after it; continuously covered customers keep the plain average | OWN | engine::optimiser, python/economics trigger_pricing |
| R33 | Rating uses market factors and the customer's individual exposure: any objective factor with an industry rating formula, registered in `config/rating_factors.yaml`. Protected attributes only as R19 allows; a factor the customer has not disclosed or consented to (R20) is rated at the average of customers who also withheld it (the undeclared group), credibility-blended with the portfolio average until that group has enough data; AI may propose an outlook, never a rate | OWN | engine::optimiser, engine::config |
| R34 | The customer allocates the plan price across covers each policy period, in any split, including all of it to one cover up to that cover's product limit; an unchanged premium allocation renews automatically and its sums insured are re-rated at the new policy period's rates | OWN | engine::consent, engine::optimiser |
| R35 | New sums insured are shown `renewal_notice_days` before the policy period starts, each with a one-sentence reason (R23), so the customer can reallocate, change plan or cancel | OWN | engine::consent, ai::explain |
| R36 | Every prevention task, verified prevention activity, point and reward belongs to the class of business of the cover it protects; behaviour changes rewards and terms only within its points pool (`points_pool_depth` in `config/lines_of_business.yaml`), never across points pools | OWN | engine::prevention, engine::score, engine::rewards |
| R37 | Classes of business form a closed tree defined in `config/lines_of_business.yaml`, validated at load; every cover names exactly one class of business; an id once used is never renamed or reused | OWN | engine::config |
| R38 | Prevention tasks target only loss-based (indemnity) covers; behaviour cannot change a parametric trigger, so parametric covers move only with risk (R31) | DER | engine::prevention, ai::prevention |
| R39 | Each policy period the customer is re-underwritten per class of business into a risk class (preferred, standard, substandard, postponed) by comparing their rated relativity, including claims experience, with the insurer's standard underwriting thresholds in config. Claims experience is evidence about the customer's own exposure, whoever caused the loss: their paid claims and payouts in that class over `claims_experience_periods` are compared with peers of the same rated exposure in the same periods, so a shock that hits everyone (a typhoon) cancels out, while a customer who claims more than peers (frequent flight-delay payouts from frequent travel) does not. Only frequency above `claims_experience_threshold` counts, credibility-weighted; claims under review count only once decided. Experience in one class affects another only through a configured, data-supported link between them (`experience_links`) | OWN | engine::optimiser |
| R40 | A prevention task's reward for a customer is `reward_share_of_claims_avoided` × the claims it avoids for that customer (the measure's claim probabilities × their rated relativity in that class, R39, at most 100%; the class's average claim cost, at most their sum insured, R48), rounded down to the cent, so it rises where prevention pays most and falls where it pays little; offered only to a customer holding the cover, always below the claims avoided (R18) and within the monthly cap (R17) | OWN | engine::prevention, engine::rewards |
| R41 | The policy renews every policy period; only the customer's cancellation ends it. A customer rated postponed in a class of business receives advance notice that the class will be excluded from their schedule. Cover in that class continues for `exclusion_notice_periods` policy periods; the exclusion then applies only if that period's re-underwriting still rates the class postponed (R39). While excluded, no premium can be allocated to it for `postponement_periods` policy periods, and the customer chooses among classes where their risk is not high (ADR-003). Open claims are unaffected; the premium is reallocated by the optimiser's recommendation unless the customer reallocates; the reason is given in one sentence (R23) and can be appealed to a person (R15). The risk class is reassessed when the postponement ends | OWN | engine::catalogue, engine::consent, engine::optimiser |
| R42 | A customer never pays for cover they cannot use. When usable cover runs out before the plan price does, the optimiser's default places the rest where the plan's limits allow, and the customer is asked to update the allocation in the app or web, shown the other covers with room and what makes each useful (a trip, a ticket, a device); without an update before the policy period starts, the default applies (R03, R34, R35). Only if the remaining covers' limits cannot take the plan price in any split (for example after postponements) may the customer change plan or cancel without penalty before the policy period starts; the system says so but never recommends a plan (R45) | OWN | engine::consent, engine::optimiser |
| R43 | Rating factors are `industry` (in the insurer's filed rating basis) or `novel` (data the industry does not collect). A novel factor starts as `candidate`: collected with consent and rated in shadow only, never affecting terms, until back-testing on pilot data shows predictive lift and no proxy for ethnicity, religion or nationality, and the insurer's actuary approves it. Behaviour already rewarded through points (R11, R40) is not also a rating factor | OWN | engine::config, python/economics, learning |
| R44 | The plan price is reduced only by a promotional discount: a time-limited campaign defined in config (period, eligible plans, discount, funder), applied deterministically to every eligible customer. Eligibility by channel, plan or new-customer status only, never by risk class or protected attributes; never set by AI | OWN | engine::plans |
| R45 | The customer chooses the plan; the system never recommends one. Plans differ in price scale and may carry privileges set in config (more reward families, more covers or event types); the optimiser works only inside the chosen plan | OWN | engine::plans |
| R46 | An urgent case (injury, hospitalisation, emergency abroad, or a loss large against the customer's income) gets the configured first response automatically within `urgent_response_seconds`, before and without waiting for a person. An automatic guarantee of payment is limited to `auto_guarantee_cap` per case and to an active cover in that class; above it the assistance provider confirms. A person is then offered and the customer chooses. Outside emergencies, a case goes to a person when the customer needs human care (signs of distress or vulnerability) or asks for one. The AI may mark a case urgent or route it to a person, never the reverse. The triage is computed when the case is notified; a loss is large at `large_loss_share_of_monthly_income` of the customer's declared earnings (ADR-010) | OWN | engine::guardrails, engine::claims, ai::assess, ai::harness |
| R47 | When a postponement ends, cover in that class starts again only after `reentry_waiting_period_days`; claims arising in the waiting period are not covered | OWN | engine::catalogue, engine::claims |
| R48 | Indemnity covers settle on a first-loss basis: a loss is paid in full up to the sum insured, with no average clause for underinsurance. Straight through, the loss is the amount claimed; a person approves the loss they assessed; either way at most the sum insured, never an AI estimate (R02, R57). When a sum insured falls below the declared value of the insured item, the renewal notice says so (R35) | OWN | engine::claims, engine::consent |
| R49 | The optimiser's suggested allocation is product information issued under the insurer's licence: it shows the factors behind it in plain words (R23), the customer can change any part of it, and what was shown is recorded: every quote is kept as shown, with the suggestion, its factors, the sentences and the price (ADR-011). It never suggests a plan (R45) | OWN | engine::optimiser, ai::explain, api (quotes in the policy store) |
| R50 | A premium rebate is funded by the insurer, set by rule in the product terms and applied identically to every customer who earns it; no intermediary can grant, vary or share it | OWN | engine::score, engine::rewards |
| R51 | Parametric payouts for one loss occurrence are bounded by its peril's `event_limit`, set from the insurer's reinsurance; a loss occurrence is the peril's trigger events in one zone, each starting within `aggregation_hours` of the last ending (a typhoon and its rain are one). A peril counted `across_zones` is one loss occurrence for the whole territory however many areas announce, as the reinsurance event definition counts one typhoon; each customer is owed only for the events in the zones they declared (ADR-012). Each payout has two stages: the guaranteed share (event limit ÷ benefit in force for the occurrence, at most 100%) is paid at once; the balance is paid when the occurrence closes, reduced pro rata if total payouts would exceed the limit. New premium allocation into a trigger zone stops while its benefit in force exceeds `zone_accumulation_limit`, as measured at most `refresh_minutes` before (ADR-012). Both limits are stated in the key features | OWN | engine::payouts, engine::catalogue |
| R52 | A policy is bound only through a distribution partner whose licence (type, number, expiry) is recorded in config and valid on that date; an unlicensed partner may only refer the customer to the insurer's own journey. Checked on the day of the customer's tap (R58) | OWN | engine::consent, config, api |
| R53 | An unpaid premium starts a grace period of `grace_period_days`: cover continues, and a claim in it is paid net of the premium due. Unpaid at its end, the policy lapses. Payment within `reinstatement_days` of the lapse reinstates it without new waiting periods; later, it is a new policy and waiting periods apply (R07, R47). No late fee; points stop fading and are kept while lapsed | OWN | engine::consent, engine::payouts, engine::score |
| R54 | On cancellation, optional data under R20 (trip data, diagnostics, calendar, task photos) is deleted, by the next daily erasure job, which keeps a record of what it erased (ADR-012). Policy, premium, claims, ledger and offer records (R49) are kept for `record_retention_years`, access-restricted and used only for compliance and underwriting. A returning customer's earlier claims count in R39 only within `claims_experience_periods` of cancellation, and the customer is told | OWN | learning, ledger, engine::consent, engine::optimiser |
| R55 | A parametric cover pays only on registered trigger event types (`config/event_types.yaml`), each with ordered stages, a measure and a zone unit, and fed by registered data sources (`config/triggers/*.yaml`) that map their own codes to an event type and stage. An event reported per zone pays only the customers whose declaration names it: a route, a ticket or booking, or a work area (a district, county or city where the declared hours are worked; ADR-012). An event type id names one peril in every market; its stages are the market's official scale. An event announced for later is in force from its start, not its announcement. Codes, stages, thresholds and the kinds of things a customer can declare (`config/exposure_types.yaml`, `config/data_types.yaml`) live in config, never in code, and are validated at load. A cover is offered only when every event type it uses has an available data source and a stated pricing basis: a registered source's historical record, or the insurer's filed rate | OWN | engine::config, engine::triggers, engine::catalogue |
| R56 | A supplier delivering settlement in kind or a partner reward is paid only for what it delivered: the agreed tariff, or its invoice up to the amount reserved, on one statement per supplier and month. The rest of an invoiced payout is paid to the customer in cash. Anything not delivered by its expiry (`valid_days`) goes back to the customer: a payout as cash, a reward to the reward balance (ADR-009) | DER | engine::suppliers, ledger, workers::suppliers |
| R57 | An indemnity claim is approved without a person (straight-through processing) only when its cover is in force for the loss and past its waiting periods (R07, R47, R53), no human-care need is seen (R46), its anomaly score is below `risk_review_threshold` (R14), the assessor recommends approve with confidence at least `claims.stp_min_confidence`, the amount claimed is within `claims.estimate_tolerance` of the assessor's estimate, the claim payment is at most `claims.stp_limit`, and it is not drawn for audit. Otherwise a person decides within `claims.referral_sla_hours`, with the reason. Every notification of loss is registered, and parametric covers take none (R08) (ADR-010) | DER | engine::claims, ai::assess, workers::claims |
| R58 | New business is bound only by the customer's tap on a quote made that market-local day, for a schedule of bundled covers totalling the plan price (R24, R34), through a partner licensed that day or the insurer's own journey (R52). Cover starts at 00:00 local the next day and every waiting period runs from then (R07); a quote binds once (ADR-011) | DER | engine::consent, api |
| R59 | A pilot's gates are fixed before it starts (`pilot.gates_fixed_on`). During the pilot, a share `pilot.control.share` of customers bound in it, drawn from the customer id and the insurer's recorded seed, is offered no prevention tasks for their first `pilot.control.periods` policy periods, then joins (a wait-list control); everything else is the same and the key features say so. Claim frequency on loss-based covers is compared between the groups for the claims avoided (ADR-012) | OWN | engine::pilot, engine::prevention, learning::pilot |
